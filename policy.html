Donation Policy

Effective Date: 8 September 2026

Voice of Ex-Servicemen is committed to maintaining transparency and accountability in receiving and utilising donations. This Donation Policy explains the terms and conditions applicable to donations made through our website.

1. Acceptance of Donations

Voice of Ex-Servicemen accepts voluntary donations from individuals and organisations through the payment methods made available on our website. Donations are intended to support activities and initiatives consistent with the aims and objectives of the organisation.

2. Voluntary Contributions

All donations made through this website are voluntary contributions. Donors are requested to carefully review the donation amount and payment details before completing a transaction.

3. Online Payment Processing

Online donations may be processed through third-party payment gateways and payment service providers. Voice of Ex-Servicemen does not request or directly store sensitive payment credentials such as debit card PINs, credit card CVVs, UPI PINs, passwords, or internet banking passwords.

Payment transactions are subject to the terms, conditions and privacy policies of the relevant payment gateway, bank, card network, UPI service provider, or other financial institution involved in the transaction.

4. Donation Confirmation

A donation will be considered successfully received after the transaction has been successfully processed and the corresponding amount has been credited to the organisation or otherwise confirmed through the applicable payment system.

Where applicable, a donation acknowledgement or receipt may be sent to the email address or other contact details provided by the donor.

5. Failed or Incomplete Transactions

If a transaction fails or is cancelled before the donation is successfully received by the organisation, the donation will not be considered completed.

If a donor's bank account or payment instrument has been debited but the organisation has not received the corresponding amount, the donor may contact us with the relevant transaction details so that the transaction can be verified.

6. Use of Donations

Donations may be used to support activities consistent with the aims and objectives of Voice of Ex-Servicemen, including welfare activities, awareness programmes, assistance initiatives, advocacy, organisational activities, and reasonable administrative and operational expenses.

Unless a specific purpose has been expressly accepted by the organisation, donations may be utilised according to the organisation's current priorities and requirements.

7. Donor Information

Information collected during the donation process will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

8. Tax Receipts and Benefits

Where applicable, donation receipts or other relevant documentation may be issued in accordance with the organisation's status, eligibility and applicable laws.

Donors are responsible for providing accurate information required for the issuance of any receipt or applicable documentation.

9. Fraudulent or Unauthorised Transactions

Voice of Ex-Servicemen reserves the right to investigate transactions that appear to be fraudulent, unauthorised, suspicious, or otherwise irregular. Where necessary, such matters may be referred to the relevant payment service provider, bank, or competent authority.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Donation Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page along with the revised effective date.

11. Contact Us

For questions regarding donations, payment transactions, receipts, or this Donation Policy, please contact us through ourContact Us page.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 8 September 2026

Voice of Ex-Servicemen appreciates the support provided by donors through our website. This policy explains the terms applicable to cancellation and refund requests relating to online donations.

1. General Policy

Donations made voluntarily through the Voice of Ex-Servicemen website are generally non-refundable and cannot be cancelledonce the donation transaction has been successfully completed.

Donors are therefore requested to carefully verify the donation amount and payment details before completing the transaction.

2. Exceptional Refund Requests

In exceptional circumstances, a donor may contact us regarding a refund where the transaction appears to have occurred due to a genuine technical error, duplicate payment, or other verifiable issue.

Each request will be reviewed individually. A refund, where approved, will be subject to verification of the transaction and the applicable procedures of the payment gateway, bank, and other payment service providers involved.

3. Duplicate Transactions

If a donor believes that the same donation has been charged more than once, the donor should contact us as soon as reasonably possible and provide the relevant transaction details.

After verification, any eligible duplicate payment may be considered for refund through the applicable payment channel.

4. Failed Transactions

If a payment transaction fails and the donation is not received by Voice of Ex-Servicemen, no donation will be considered successfully completed.

If the donor's account has nevertheless been debited, the donor should first allow reasonable time for the payment system or bank to reconcile the transaction. If the amount is not automatically reversed or settled, the donor may contact us with the transaction details for verification.

5. Refund Method

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be processed through the original payment method or through the payment gateway's applicable refund mechanism.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in the donor's account may depend on the donor's bank, card issuer, UPI service provider, payment gateway, or other financial institution.

6. Information Required for Verification

To investigate a refund request, we may require information such as the donor's name, date of transaction, donation amount, transaction ID, payment reference number, and contact details used during the transaction.

Donors should never provide their card PIN, CVV, UPI PIN, password, banking password, or other confidential payment credentials to us.

7. Contact for Refund Requests

Refund-related requests should be submitted through ourContact Us page with sufficient transaction details to allow us to verify the payment.

8. Changes to This Policy

Voice of Ex-Servicemen reserves the right to modify this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with the revised effective date.
