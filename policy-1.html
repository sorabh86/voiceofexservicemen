Donation Policy

Voice of Ex-Servicemen is committed to maintaining transparency and accountability in the receipt and utilisation of donations. This Donation Policy explains the terms and conditions applicable to donations made through our website.

1. Acceptance of Donations

Voice of Ex-Servicemen accepts voluntary donations from individuals and organisations through the payment methods made available on our website. Donations are intended to support the organisation's activities, welfare initiatives, awareness programmes, administrative requirements, and other legitimate objectives of the organisation.

2. Voluntary Contribution

All donations made through this website are voluntary contributions. A donor should make a donation only after carefully reviewing the amount and payment details before completing the transaction.

3. Online Payment

Online donations may be processed through third-party payment gateway providers. Voice of Ex-Servicemen does not directly store or have access to sensitive payment information such as debit card numbers, credit card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, or internet banking passwords.

Donors are required to provide accurate information during the donation process. The processing of payments is subject to the terms and policies of the applicable payment gateway and the donor's bank or financial institution.

4. Donation Confirmation

Once a successful donation transaction is received and confirmed by the payment gateway, a confirmation or acknowledgement may be provided to the donor through the email address or other contact information submitted during the donation process.

A payment transaction shown as successful by a bank or payment service does not necessarily mean that the donation has been received by the organisation. The donation will be considered received only after confirmation of the transaction by the applicable payment system or our records.

5. Failed or Incomplete Transactions

If an online donation transaction fails, is cancelled, or remains incomplete, no donation will be considered successfully received unless the amount is subsequently credited to the organisation's account.

In cases where the donor's account has been debited but the donation has not been credited to the organisation, the donor should contact us with the relevant transaction details so that the matter can be verified.

6. Refund and Cancellation Policy

Donations made voluntarily through this website are generallynon-refundable and cannot be cancelled once the transaction has been successfully completed.

However, if a donation has been made due to a genuine technical error, duplicate transaction, or other exceptional circumstance, the donor may contact us within a reasonable period with the transaction details. Any refund request will be reviewed and processed at the sole discretion of the organisation, subject to verification and applicable payment gateway and banking procedures.

7. Duplicate Payments

If a donor believes that the same donation has been charged more than once, the donor should contact us as soon as possible with the transaction reference numbers and other relevant details. Duplicate transactions will be verified before any eligible refund is considered.

8. Use of Donations

Donations received by Voice of Ex-Servicemen may be utilised for activities consistent with the aims and objectives of the organisation. These may include Ex-Servicemen welfare activities, awareness programmes, advocacy, assistance initiatives, organisational activities, and reasonable administrative and operational expenses.

Unless a specific purpose is expressly stated and accepted by the organisation, donations may be allocated according to the organisation's current priorities and requirements.

9. Donor Information

We respect the privacy of our donors. Personal information collected during the donation process will be handled in accordance with ourPrivacy Policy.

Donors should not submit sensitive payment credentials, including card PINs, UPI PINs, passwords, or similar confidential information to us through email, forms, or other communication channels.

10. Tax Benefits and Donation Receipts

Where applicable, donation receipts or other documentation may be issued in accordance with applicable laws and the organisation's eligibility and registration status.

Donors should ensure that the information provided during the donation process, including their name, address, email address, and other required details, is accurate if they require an official receipt or documentation.

11. Fraudulent or Unauthorised Transactions

Voice of Ex-Servicemen reserves the right to investigate transactions that appear to be fraudulent, unauthorised, suspicious, or inconsistent with applicable laws and regulations.

Where necessary, suspicious transactions may be reported to the relevant payment service provider, bank, or competent authority.

12. Changes to This Policy

Voice of Ex-Servicemen reserves the right to modify or update this Donation Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with the updated effective date.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding a donation, payment transaction, refund request, or this Donation Policy, please contact us using the contact information provided on our website.
